QUESTION:
My internet connection itself is down. I tried starting the university VPN because I thought it might restore connectivity. Will that work?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['No', 'A working internet connection is required before the VPN tunnel can be established']

 A working internet connection is required before the university VPN can operate. If the VPN client repeatedly fails, the user should record the error message before contacting support.

QUESTION:
I mistyped my password several times and my account became locked about six minutes ago. Should I keep trying to sign in?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['No', 'Wait until 15 minutes have passed before trying again']

MODEL:
 If after multiple failed sign-in attempts the account remains locked, the user should contact the IT Service Desk. A temporarily locked account may be released after 15 minutes of repeated failed sign-in attempts.

====================
QUESTION:
My account has remained locked for about 30 minutes after repeated failed logins. What should I do now?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['The normal 15-minute temporary lock period has passed', 'Contact the IT Service Desk']

MODEL:
 A locked account should be left for 15 minutes before trying again. If the account remains locked after the 15-minute temporary lock period, the user should contact the IT Service Desk.
