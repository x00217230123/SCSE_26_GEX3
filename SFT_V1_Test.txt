
====================
QUESTION:
My internet connection itself is down. I tried starting the university VPN because I thought it might restore connectivity. Will that work?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['No', 'A working internet connection is required before the VPN tunnel can be established']

MODEL:
 No. A stable internet connection requires normal internet traffic rather than a working VPN tunnel. Make sure your normal internet connection works first.

====================
QUESTION:
My normal internet works, but I changed my university password this morning and the VPN now rejects my login. What should I try?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['Disconnect and reconnect the VPN', 'Use the updated university credentials']

MODEL:
 Disconnect the VPN and reconnect using your updated university credentials. Make sure your normal internet connection works first.

====================
QUESTION:
The VPN client keeps failing and I am about to contact support. What information should I record first?

EXPECTED POINTS:
["Record the VPN client's error message"]

MODEL:
 First record the error message in the VPN tunnel and the name of the VPN server you are using. Do not disconnect the VPN until you are sure the connection is stable.

QUESTION:
I submitted a print job at 4 PM yesterday and forgot to release it. It is 3 PM today. Should it normally still be available?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['Yes', 'Less than 24 hours have passed', 'Unreleased print jobs are retained for up to 24 hours']

MODEL:
 No. A forgotten print job should not be submitted to the queue. The job is now released, but it is not yet ready for printing.

====================
QUESTION:
I submitted a print job at 4 PM yesterday and it is now 5 PM today. I never released it. Should I expect it to still be there?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['No', 'More than 24 hours have passed', 'The unreleased job should have been automatically deleted', 'Submit the document again']

MODEL:
 No. A print job is automatically deleted after 24 hours.

QUESTION:
My friend is visiting campus and cannot use Campus-Secure. Should I give them my university username and password so they can connect?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['No', 'Do not share university credentials', 'The visitor should use Campus-Guest']

QUESTION:
I have a large research dataset containing sensitive university information. My personal cloud account has plenty of space. Is it suitable?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['No', 'Use approved university research storage', 'Sensitive university data should be stored only in approved university services']

MODEL:
 No. A large research dataset should not be stored on a personal cloud account, especially when containing sensitive university information. Use approved research storage rather than personal cloud folders, particularly when the dataset is sensitive or contains university data.
